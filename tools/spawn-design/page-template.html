Greater Britain Spawn Plan

Every creature spawner within about 500 tiles of the Britain bank, as stock world generation made it ("Current") and as the areas now theme it. Click a spawner, an area or a flavor object on the map to see what it holds. Deployed to the dev world on 18 October 2026 (ShardContent efdf65c), with 316 flavor objects and Felucca in summer.

What was decided

  1. The graveyard keeps only the lesser dead. Skeletons, zombies and headless ones. Its spectres and wraiths now walk Barrow Fields just north of it (your map comment), a step-up area and an exception to the rings, and armoured undead walk the Haunted Wood south-west.
  2. The beginner sites lost their step-up creatures. Graded by their worst, ratmen, orcs and lizardmen made them tier 2 at Britain's doorstep; they live next door in their own areas instead (West Foothills, North Hills, Bay Marsh). This replaced the 15 October "sprinkle in" ruling.
  3. Your map comments. The woods by the bay are beginner (Bay Woods); west of the mountains is tier 3 (West Mountain Woods, with two new deep-forest spawners) and the east part joined the South-west Farmlands.
  4. Wildlife stays, graded by ring. The 19 stock wildlife spawners keep their places with a roster for their ring (deer and small game near town; wolves, bears and big cats further out; grizzlies only in the far wilds), and 24 new sites fill the forests that had none.
  5. Flavor objects, not scenes. Composed scenes looked illogical (debris on a log, a lone fence), so each area has single standalone pieces instead, on clean open ground, never a player item.
  6. Felucca in summer. Leaves on the trees and flowers in the fields, as Britannia looked before Trammel, instead of the stock Desolation season (worldSeason.felucca in shard-rules.json).
  7. How it reached the world. A shard spawn file that reuses the stock GUIDs and a flavor decoration file; [Alpha2bWorldGen addspawners and adddecorations put them into the dev world after a save snapshot. The audit passes with 1,583 spawners. The bot farm's host still has the old spawns.

Sources: the generated spawn data as deployed (Distribution/Data/BritanniaRenaissance/Alpha2b/Spawns, last record per GUID wins, as world generation does), creature hit points and damage from the stock classes, bot deaths from the bot farm's shared memory (runs 1 and 2b, 40-tile cells). Tiers: 0 harmless, 1 beginner (up to about 60 hit points and 10 a hit), 2 step-up (60-75 hit points, or poison), 3 dangerous (75-130 hit points, or casts), 4 deadly. The sea and the farms' cats, dogs and chickens are left as they are. Tools: ShardContent/tools/spawn-design.
