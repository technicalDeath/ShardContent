## Verified, left alone (the coordinator's list)

1. **Family topics inherited by derived weapons** (TopicFor walks base classes). I took every derived class of my `types` (list in the notes) and checked whether it can exist in this world. Obtainable: **JukaBow** (row 7) and **GlacialStaff** (ice serpent, 2.5%, `IceSerpent.cs:58-61`; spawns in Ice, Outdoors, LostLands). GlacialStaff is a Black staff with a hue and a name; its AOS properties are inert here (`GlacialStaff.cs`: "TODO: Pre-AoS stuff"), so w-staff is true for it. Not obtainable: DemonKnight, Leviathan, champion-spawn bosses and Ilhenir/Twaulo drops (none in the spawn data), paragons (`Paragon.Maps = Ilshenar` only), treasure-map artifacts (level 6 and `Core.AOS` only, `TreasureMapChest.cs:305`), stealable artifacts (all Malas entries, and the map is off), New Haven quest rewards (ML quests off), Heritage gifts. Gargoyle's pickaxe (in the pickaxe topic): `Gargoyle.cs:42-45` 2.5%, Gargoyle spawns in Outdoors (54) and Hythloth (14).
2. **Quivers**: confirmed not obtainable, topic deleted (row 12).
3. **SBWeaponSmith 1-in-3**: confirmed (row 4). Hatchets, War fork and the staves/clubs/maces/spears are in the always-on part (`SBWeaponSmith.cs:16-37`), so the other topics' "weaponsmiths sell" sentences are right. Blacksmith pointer: right, `SBBlacksmith` has every weapon in the topics.
4. **Where each family is sold in Britain** (box x 1280-1750, y 1400-1900, `Spawns/shared/felucca/Vendors.json` and `Alpha2b/Spawns/Vendors.json` agree; `britain-vendor-overrides.json` only has Herbalist/Alchemist/Mage): Blacksmith 2, Weaponsmith 4, Bowyer 1, Provisioner 2, Carpenter 1 (and a Butcher 1, who also sells the Butcher knife for 13, not claimed). No Tinker, Miner, Rancher, Variety Dealer. Every "Blacksmiths/weaponsmiths/bowyers/carpenters/provisioners sell" sentence names a vendor that spawns there and a list that holds the item at the stated price. The sentences "a tinker makes..." are player crafting, not a shop. Prices checked against `SBBlacksmith.cs`, `SBWeaponSmith.cs`, `Weapons/SBRangedWeapon.cs`, `Weapons/SBStavesWeapon.cs`, `SBProvisioner.cs:27-28` (arrows 2/bolts 5, 20 each); bowyer arrows 2/bolts 3 with `Random(30,60)` = 30-89 (`SBRangedWeapon.cs:24-26`). `GenericBuyInfo.Price` returns the listed price when the scalar is 0 (`GenericBuy.cs:56-80`), i.e. the numbers are exact.
5. **Numbers and tooltips**: shard has no tooltips (`opl.enable` not set), all numbers in the topics are the stock `Old*` values (`BaseWeapon.cs:310-331,508-520,578-597`); no shard code changes weapon stats (only `NoviceCombatService`, `PostUorSystemGates`, `StarterCombatIssuance` touch weapons). ## OWNER

- OWNER: Weaponsmith stock is randomised in stock code (`SBWeaponSmith.cs:39`): only about 1 in 3 weaponsmiths (4 spawn in Britain) sell swords, axes, pole arms, bows, crossbows or knives, and the roll is redone whenever the vendor loads. The text now says "go to a Blacksmith". If the owner wants weaponsmiths to be a reliable shop, that is a code/data change, not a text one.
- OWNER: An exceptional pickaxe made in this era very likely has 150 uses, not 100 (BaseAxe doubles the uses when the quality is set, then `Pickaxe.OnCraft` adds 50; the upstream "+50 for UOR" fix did not account for the doubling). I avoided the number. Needs a one-line live check (`TestOnlyProbe` shows `usesRemaining`) before anyone writes it down.
- OWNER: Under the T2A craft menus only the Blacksmith menu has Repair (`BlacksmithMenu.cs:214`; the Tinkering, Carpentry and Bowcraft menus have none, and `RepairDeed` is not in this world). So bows, crossbows, staves, clubs, the crook, hatchets, pickaxes and knives can never be repaired by a player. The text only says what a Blacksmith can repair and does not promise anything else; say so if the owner wants it stated or changed.
- OWNER: The new gated sentence in w-weapon hard-codes the NoviceCombat floors (60 and 30), as the fighting pages do. If `noviceCombat.staminaFloor` / `tacticsFloor` change in `shard-rules.json`, both places need the new numbers.
