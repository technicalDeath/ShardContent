# Audit: Fighting Skill Guide pages (Wrestling, Swords, Macing, Fencing, Archery, Parry, Tactics, Anatomy, Healing)

File audited and fixed in place: `parts/fighting.skills.json` (valid JSON, ASCII only, every page keeps `stats` + `gain`, 5 headings, 2,361 to 5,252 characters of own text). Original kept in the audit scratchpad only. Coordinator decisions 1 to 6 applied.

## Counts

- Text segments checked: about 540 sentences or list lines (roughly 230 distinct once the paragraphs repeated across the five weapon pages are counted once). Every number was re-derived from code or config.
- Wrong, corrected: 12 groups (33 edits across pages), see the table.
- Removed as unsupported advice (decision 2) or as duplicating the generated Stats block (decision 1): 9 groups.
- Left alone after verification: everything else, notably all weapon damage/speed/Strength numbers, every price, hit chance and damage formulas, shield tables, bandage formulas, the Poison Relief / NoviceCombat / Faint Memories figures, teachers, spawns.

## Verified and left alone (highlights)

- All 28 weapon numbers (damage, speed, Strength, range, hands) and the swing times: per-weapon `Old*` values; hands from `Layer` in code or the client tiledata.mul in `UOData` (I re-read the file: Axe, Halberd, staves, Short Spear, Spear, Pitchfork two-handed; Katana, Kryss, Club, Mace, Dagger, swords, War Mace, Maul, War Fork, War Axe one-handed; Battle Axe, War Hammer, bows two-handed and Hammer Pick one-handed from code).
- Prices: SBBlacksmith (weapons, shields), SBRangedWeapon (Bowyer bow 40, crossbow 55, arrows 2, bolts 3), SBProvisioner (arrows 2, bolts 5), SBHealer (bandage 5), SBTailor (cloth 2, scissors 11, bolt 100), SBAlchemist (Lesser Cure 15); list price is the price (`GenericBuy.Price` returns the list price when the scalar is 0 or 100).
- Hit chance, no-challenge rule, damage formula (Tactics, Strength, Anatomy, Lumberjacking on axes only, Exceptional +20%, halved against players), durability wear (1 in 25 damaging hits, half damage at worst), mace/staff stamina drain 3 to 5 (War Axe is `Bashing` by type but a `BaseAxe`, no drain), Fists 1-8 speed 30, Stun/Disarm disabled messages, no automatic procs (shard-rules.json `combat`).
- Archery: ranges 10/8, stationary delay 1.0 s to 0.5 s (0.95 s at Dex 33), ammo use, 40% arrow recovery on a miss and 40% arrow in a monster's pack on a hit, butte rules (5 or 6 tiles, 2 s, gather within 1 tile), dummy rules (3 s swing, refuses at 25.0 and on a mount); buttes and dummies exist in Britain's decoration data.
- Parry formulas and the table (all four shields at Parry 50 and 100 recomputed), shield Strength 0, durability 1 block in 8.
- Bandage: ranges, self wait 9.4 + 0.6 x ((120 - Dex)/10), others 5/4/3 s (+5 dead), heal formula and examples, slips, cure and resurrection chances (Healing - 30)/50 and (Healing - 68)/50, thresholds 60/80, poison levels 0 to 4, bandage used up on failure, heal potions refused when poisoned.
- Anatomy: use range 8, 1 s delay, margin of error, stamina reading at Base 65, no extra at Grandmaster (UOTD off), skills-window button (ClassicUO StandardSkillsGump.cs:794-802, 845-848).
- Gated paragraphs: NoviceCombat (0.66, Tactics 30, stamina 60, any target, players only, gain unchanged), PoisonRelief (5 potions at creation, hint once per character below 60, 30 s grace for Lesser from non-player sources), FaintMemories (0.2 step, 10.0 to 80.0, 24 h), Alpha3StarterCombatGear (Katana/Club/Kryss/Bow, shield from Parry, 50 bandages, 100 arrows, leather chest and legs), SafeWorld/HotZones/KnockedOut text matches `WelcomeGuide.cs`. All flag names exist in `DeferredFeatureFlags`; no off flag and no anti-macro is mentioned.
- Teachers (decision 3), all present in the spawn data (`Alpha2b/Spawns` after the world-generation filters): Blacksmith x2, Warrior Guildmaster, Mage Guildmaster x3, Bard Guildmaster, Healer + Healer Guildmaster (one spawner), Bowyer, 179 Wandering Healer spawners. Skill ranges give the printed "about N to M" figures (BaseCreature.cs:2849-2870, 4736-4775).
- Monster hit points and Wrestling ranges: from `SetHits`/`SetSkill` (not from ShardContent docs, decision 5); e.g. Orc 58 to 72, Wrestling 50.1 to 70.

## OWNER

OWNER: Weaponsmiths (stock ModernUO) carry the sword/axe/bow/dagger/kryss stock in only about one of three vendors (`Utility.Random(3)` with a single `case 0`, rolled at each vendor's creation). The pages say "only some of the time" and point to the Blacksmith; say if you want this made fixed.
OWNER: Wooden and ranged weapons (Club, staves, bows, crossbows) cannot be repaired at all in UOR (Carpentry and Bowcraft have `Repair = Core.AOS`), while a worn weapon loses up to half its damage. The Macing page now says so; confirm that is intended.
OWNER: The Welcome guide's "Who teaches what" list omits the Guildmasters (Warrior, Mage, Bard, Healer) and Wandering Healers, who do teach (Wrestling from the Mage Guildmaster, Parry/Tactics/Swords from the Warrior Guildmaster, Anatomy and Healing from the Healer Guildmaster and Wandering Healers). The pages name them; reconcile the two lists or tell me to drop them.
