# Audit: Alchemy slice (`base/skills.json` Alchemy page and `base/items.json`, 14 topics)

Independent pass on the coordinator's own text. Every sentence was re-read against the code that runs (UOR, `Core.UOTD` false so the T2A packet craft menus are on; `expansion.json` is Renaissance). Both files were edited in place; they parse, are ASCII only, have no banned words, every title is 18 characters or fewer, each topic has 4 or fewer links, every `related` key exists, every class name is in one topic only, and the Alchemy page keeps 4 headings, about 1,970 characters of own text and the `craft:Alchemy`, `stats` and `gain` blocks. The `categories` list is untouched and every topic key is kept.

## Counts

- Checked: 1 skill page (13 paragraphs, about 45 claims) and 14 item topics (about 70 sentences, about 190 claims: every number, delay, price, vendor, recipe, flow step and client behavior).
- Wrong and fixed: 7 (failure loss, keg recipe, explosion "no sides", cure potion "surest", poison weapon rule and "a Cure potion cures it", refresh "a quarter of your stamina", reagents page shown for non-reagent classes).
- Misleading and fixed: 6 (general potion page, "its name tells you what it is", "everything has weight", "a tinker" read as an NPC, drink-with-a-free-hand omission on 6 topics, empty bottle not returned by thrown explosion potions).
- Removed as unverified or out of era: 1 ("Its name tells you what it is.").
- Left alone: everything else (all heal/cure/agility/strength/explosion numbers, 10 second delay, 100-potion keg, weights, all prices, the flow text, the night sight duration).

## Verified and left unchanged (main items)

- **Flow** (point 1 of the assignment). `BaseTool.OnDoubleClick` (`Items/Skill Items/Tools/BaseTool.cs:154-167`): in the pack, `T2ACraftSystem.Enabled` (`ExpansionConfiguration.cs:15`, `!Core.UOTD`, `expansion.json` is Renaissance) gives the cursor and the message "Target this tool to make last item, or any other target to begin crafting." `T2ACraftToolTarget.cs:19-69`: the tool itself repeats the last potion (or "You have not yet crafted anything." and the menu), anything else opens the menu. `T2ACraftSystem.cs:40-51`: a `BaseReagent` or `Bottle` (or a non-item target) opens `AlchemyMenu` straight away, any other item asks "Target a reagent or empty bottle." again. `AlchemyMenu.cs`: category menu "What kind of potion?" then "Which potion would you like to make?", both filtered by `T2ACraftSystem.CanCraftItem` (skill at least the low number with success chance above 0, plus the bottle and the reagent in the pack). All 21 era potions are offered; the SE potions in `DefAlchemy.cs:258` are not built. The page text matches. Worn tool: `BaseTool.UsesRemaining--` on every attempt, success or failure (`CraftItem.cs:1411,1611`), deleted at 0 (`BreakOnDepletion`).
- **Training sentences.** Success chance is `(skill - min) / (max - min)` with `GetChanceAtMin => 0.0` (`CraftItem.cs:900-905`, `DefAlchemy.cs:30`), 0 below the low number (`allRequiredSkills`), a certain success at the high number, so a high number above 100 is never certain at Grandmaster. Gain: `SkillCheck.CheckLocation` returns false below `min` and true without a gain roll at `value >= max` (`SkillCheck.cs:64-76`); the shard's Mastery and curve hooks (`SkillEvents.InvokeSkillGainOverride`, `InvokeGainChanceMultiplier`) sit inside `CheckSkill`, after that early return, so "past the second number it no longer teaches you" holds with Mastery on.
- **Heal.** 3-10 / 6-20 / 9-30 (`Core.AOS` false), `Delay => 10.0` for all three, one `BeginAction<BaseHealPotion>` shared by the three, full health refused, poisoned refused ("You can not heal yourself in your current state."), `Heal` goes through `Mobile.Heal` unchanged (only the knocked-out service blocks it).
- **Cure.** All three non-AOS tables in `LesserCurePotion`, `CurePotion`, `GreaterCurePotion` equal the page (75/50/15; 100/75/50/15; 100/100/100/75/25). Not poisoned: "You are not poisoned." and the potion is kept. Failure message 500232. Bandage: `Bandage.cs:315-322` (Healing and Anatomy at least 60).
- **Agility and Strength.** +10 / +20 Dex or Str for 2.0 minutes (`BaseAgilityPotion`, `BaseStrengthPotion`; `Scale` is the identity outside AOS). Strength: `HitsMax` in `PlayerMobile.cs`, `MaxWeight => 40 + 3.5 * Str` (`PlayerMobile.cs:239`), `BaseWeapon.ScaleDamageOld` (1 percent per 5 Str). Dex: `StamMax => Dex` (`Mobile.cs:2166`).
- **Explosion.** Ranges 5-10 / 10-20 / 15-30 (`GreaterExplosionPotion` non-AOS), alchemy bonus `Alchemy / 10` (direct only), cap 40 (`!Core.AOS && damage > 40`), radius `ExplosionRange = 2`, throw range 10 (`Core.ML ? 12 : 10`), fuse 0.75 s + 3 x 1.0 s (about 3.75 s, counted 3-2-1 overhead), damage goes through `AOS.Damage` which is plain `m.Damage` outside AOS (no resist).
- **Night sight.** `NightSightTimer` uses `Utility.Random(15, 25)` (`LightCycle.cs:115`), and `Utility.Random(from, count)` is `from + Next(count)` (`BuiltInRng.cs:34`), so 15 to 39 whole minutes: the new text is right. Light: personal level `DungeonLevel / 2` = 13 (`NightSight.cs`); in ClassicUO the darkness pass is skipped when `Personal >= Overall` (`GameScene.cs:81-85`, `1132`), which removes night darkness (global 12) entirely, and in a dungeon (global 26) the ambient level becomes `max(13, 32-26)` (`IsometricLight.cs:67-72`): "night and dungeon darkness are much lighter" holds.
- **Keg.** `OnDragDrop` and `OnDoubleClick` (`PotionKeg.cs`), capacity 100, one kind, bottles returned, weight `20 + Held * 0.8` (`DefaultWeight`). Dragging to the keg works in ClassicUO because tiledata 0x1940 carries the Container flag (read from `UOData/tiledata.mul`) and `ContainerGump.cs:274-280` sends the drop to a container-flagged target; server side `Item.DropToItem` reaches `OnDragDrop` for a non-`Container` item.
- **Prices and vendors.** `SBAlchemist.cs` (Bottle 5, MortarPestle 8, BlackPearl 5, Bloodmoss 5, the other six 3), `SBMage.cs` (same reagent prices; necro reagents only under `Core.AOS`), `SBHerbalist.cs` (Mortar 8, Bottle 5, five reagents). Spawn: `ModernUO/Distribution/Data/BritanniaRenaissance/Alpha2b/Spawns/Vendors.json` (Britain only) holds Alchemist x3 (1498,1659; 1485,1550; 1590,1654), Mage x3, Herbalist x1 inside x 1280-1750, y 1400-1900, and `britain-vendor-overrides.json` adds Alchemist x2 (one at 1575,1703), Mage x2, Herbalist x1; no `Tinker` anywhere in Britain. `BaseVendor.GetPriceScalar` leaves the prices as listed. Monsters that carry reagents (EvilMage and others, `PackReg`) are in the spawn files.
- **Free hand and client claims.** Drag to move, drop on a pack or the ground, pile onto pile: `GameActions.PickUp`, `GameSceneInputHandler.OnLeftMouseUp`, `ContainerGump` (above); ground range 3 tiles, not stated in the text. Overweight: `StaminaSystem.ProcessPlayerMovement` (stamina drain per step, "too fatigued to move").
- **Art ids** for all 14 topics read from the item constructors (0xF06-0xF0E, 0xF7A, 0xE9B, 0x1940).

## OWNER:

1. **Free-hand rule.** Stock `BasePotion.CanDrink` refuses to drink unless one hand is free ("You must have a free hand to drink a potion."), so a warrior with a weapon and shield, or anyone holding a bow or another two-handed weapon, cannot drink a heal, cure or any other drinkable potion. The text now states this because the code does it. I could not confirm from an online source whether the rule existed in April 2000; if it does not belong to the era, the change is a `RequireFreeHand` override in the shard, and the six sentences should then be deleted.
2. **Melisande's Fermented Wine** (`MelisandesFermentedWine`, a later-era quest item derived from `GreaterExplosionPotion`) resolves to the explosion page because the topic walk finds the exact base class first. It is unobtainable here (needs the later-era check in `CanDrink`), so I left it; adding its name to the `potion` topic's `types` would send it to the general potion page.
