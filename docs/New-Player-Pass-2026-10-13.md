# New-player pass, 2026-10-13

Done on the owner's "find some work you can do autonomously for new players ... qol for new players", with free rein over the stack and the instruction to present it for approval afterwards. **Nothing here is committed, pushed or deployed to the dev server.** The player client in `ClassicUO/bin/dist` (`cuo.dll`, `Data/Profiles/default.json`) already has it, because that is where it was played; the backup of the previous `cuo.dll` is `work/cuo-before-newplayer-pass.dll`. No server flag was changed.

## How it was found

A brand-new character on a disposable host, played in the real ClassicUO client as a new player would, with screenshots (`work/help-gumps/seq2/`). Looked at: the first screen, the status window, the skills window, the journal, the backpack and map, fighting a rat and looting it, a vendor, the guide, dying. The findings that were client-side or text-only and needed no ruling were fixed; the rest are listed at the end.

## Not tested, or limits

- The right-hand column needs a window about 1,200 px wide; with the paperdoll in it too, only about one more window fits (the game view is 70% of the window); on a smaller window everything opens where it used to. The small map fell back to the top-left in the test because the column was already full.
- Only new profiles get the status window, the macros and the other profile defaults; a player with an existing profile keeps theirs. The open skill groups and the column layout are code, so they apply to everyone.
- Alt+Shift+B (bandage your last target) was not exercised; it uses the stock macro.
- The body strip tracks only the character's own death position and the corpses the client has seen; if the body is moved (a pet or another player dragging it) the marker stays where the character died until the body is next seen within two tiles of it. A death by something that leaves no body (a staff kill with a gate) is not special-cased. The corpse window still opens over the paperdoll (stock placement).
- The spell bar was seen on one Pure Mage only: the Shrunk and Docked states, a cast that needs a target, a spell that is not in the book, and a Magery of exactly 0 were not exercised. Mana costs are the era's circle values (4, 6, 9, 11, 14, 20, 40, 50) kept in the client, since the client has no cost table for Magery spells. Circles 3 and up are not shown (the bar would be too wide); say if you want more.
- Nothing was tested at other window sizes or with a second character.

## Found but not done (needs a ruling, or is not a small change)

1. **First death text.** The strip now covers what a ghost should do, as the client's own text; the server-side one-time message on the owner's list (`New-Player-Help-Messages.md` B4) is still awaiting approval and would now be redundant for the client text, so it may be dropped.
2. **Body marker on the map.** The strip gives direction and distance; a pin on the map is not built.
3. **The vendor and bill-of-sale windows still open on top of each other and on the journal** (stock behaviour). Docking them is possible, but a trade window should sit near the vendor.
4. **The status window still opens on top of the paperdoll from the paperdoll's own Status button** (it follows the paperdoll). Left as the stock behaviour.
5. **The item shortcut bar's first-run hint is the stock text.** A short "try dragging your bandages here" would be a client text change.
