## Focused matrix reconciliation — September 28, 2026

The accepted evidence now forms one focused Skill Bank matrix: automated policy/persistence and SkillEvents cases cover malformed data, recovery guards, organic and sub-10 deltas, non-organic exclusions, anti-macro behavior, cap exchanges, and mastery-first ordering; ordinary-client rehearsals cover bank restoration and deposits, retention, anti-macro denial, full-bank replacement, ordinary-user recovery denial, administrator recovery, and three real world-save/reload boundaries. The September 28 focused ShardContent run passed 219/219 at `work/agent-verification/runs/20260928T075246785Z-0ef67e`; the focused ledger and event runs cited above passed against isolated current ModernUO references. Source flags and acknowledgment remained off throughout. This reconciliation closes the Skill Bank matrix-validation task; it does not authorize activation or close the independent skill-by-skill progression and overall Alpha 3 release gates.

The focused UOContent `SkillEventsTests` run passed 21/21 at `work/agent-verification/runs/20260928T034906814Z-651854` (schema-2 report, current-source fingerprint). The new variable sub-10 test uses deterministic assertions over the supported random range rather than expecting one seeded outcome.

On September 27, 2026, a disposable ModernUO server loaded a copy of the accepted Alpha 2b
world on an isolated loopback listener. Its Skill Bank feature flag remained off. A temporary
administrator-only probe assembly was installed only in that disposable server to exercise the
public `SkillBankService.RestoreFromValidatedLedger` path; it was removed after the rehearsal.
The shared server and saves were not used.

The probe created one synthetic ordinary `PlayerMobile` with the shard's 700.0 active skill cap
and 100.0 individual skill cap. Anatomy and Tactics began at 50.0 each, six other skills at
100.0 each, and Tactics was Down. The bank held 0.1 Anatomy. At the full active cap, the
restoration returned `Restored`: Anatomy became 50.1, Tactics became 49.9, the active total
remained 700.0, and the bank held 0.1 Tactics instead of Anatomy.

The disposable server completed a world save, shut down and started again from that save. A
staff client then invoked the probe's verification command against the reloaded synthetic
character. It confirmed the active values, active cap, canonical bank payload, 0.1 Tactics
balance and zero Anatomy balance together. The synthetic character was deleted, its absence
was confirmed, and the disposable world was saved and shut down. The probe DLL and its
assembly-list entry were removed from the disposable host.

This service-level case proves one full-cap exchange and a world-save/restart roundtrip with both sides of the exchange in one player record. The reconciled focused matrix below and in the case table now covers organic and variable sub-10 displacement, scripted/modifier exclusions, anti-macro denial, restoration ordering and cap boundaries, malformed-data recovery, and repeated live save/reload states. Per-skill progression and time-driven Mastery award-period checks remain in the separate UOR player-skill audit. The Skill Bank flag remains disabled pending the overall Alpha 3 release gate.

## Ordinary client skill-use rehearsal

On September 27, a fresh ordinary account used Navrey on the disposable shard. A temporary
test-only command set the character to the 700.0 active cap with Anatomy at 5.0 Up, Tactics at
95.0 Down, and 0.1 Anatomy in the bank. The temporary assembly enabled `skillBank` in memory;
the source flag remained disabled. The client then used the normal Anatomy targeting action on
a reachable test rat.

The first valid use exposed duplicate `MasteryProgression.Configure` registration: it restored
the banked tenth and also allowed an ordinary stock gain. ModernUO automatically invokes every
public `Configure` method, and `ShardBootstrap.Configure` also calls the progression setup.
Adding an idempotence guard to `MasteryProgression.Configure` resolved the duplicate callback.
After rebuilding and restarting the disposable server, the same client action changed Anatomy
from 5.0 to 5.1 and Tactics from 95.0 to 94.9, moved the banked tenth from Anatomy to Tactics,
and kept the active total at 700.0. A second ordinary Anatomy use changed Anatomy to 5.2 and
Tactics to 94.8, and banked the exact second displaced tenth in Tactics. The client set Tactics'
bank retention to Down; `[SkillBank` displayed 0.2 Tactics and its separate Down state.

The client disconnected, the disposable world saved and shut down, then restarted. On login,
`[SkillBank` still displayed 0.2 Tactics, Down, alongside the 5.2/94.8 active values and 700.0
total. The test rat was deleted, the temporary flag assembly and credential file were removed,
and the scratch assembly list was restored. The 209-test ShardContent suite passed after the
registration fix. This confirms the ordinary client path and one save/restart boundary.
The scratch anti-macro configuration was disabled for this run; the separate matrix below covers
anti-macro denial and full-bank replacement. Invalid-payload recovery is recorded above in
SB-REC-CLIENT and SB-REC-MESSAGES. The later anti-macro/full-bank case and the three-boundary persistence sequence below complete the focused Skill Bank matrix; broader per-skill progression and time-driven Mastery checks remain in the separate player-skill audit.

## Anti-macro and full-bank client matrix

The disposable server ran one ordinary-client session with anti-macro enabled only in its
temporary configuration and Skill Bank enabled only in memory. A fresh test character began at
the 700.0 active cap with 0.3 Anatomy banked, Anatomy 5.0 Up, and Tactics 95.0 Down. The test
rat was placed at the character's tile so the normal Anatomy target action had valid range and
line of sight.

Two uses on that rat restored 0.1 Anatomy each and banked the matching 0.1 Tactics loss each
time. The third use on the same target reached the anti-macro allowance: it consumed no banked
Anatomy and created no bank credit. Because stock sub-10 skill gain is unconditional in this
engine branch, the active Anatomy and Tactics values still changed by 0.2 on that denied use;
the existing bank balances stayed at 0.1 Anatomy and 0.2 Tactics. A new rat target then allowed
the remaining 0.1 Anatomy to restore, leaving 0.3 Tactics banked. This separates anti-macro
eligibility for bank accounting from stock sub-10 active progression.

The same client then tested capacity using a temporary full 300.0 bank: 100.0 Alchemy, 100.0
Blacksmithy and 100.0 Mining, all Locked. An ordinary eligible Anatomy gain displaced 0.1
Tactics; the player received the full-bank warning, and all three existing balances stayed
unchanged. The player used `[SkillBank down Mining`, then made another eligible Anatomy use on
a fresh rat. That use displaced 0.3 Tactics, replaced exactly 0.3 from Mining, and left the
bank at 300.0. `[SkillBank` showed Alchemy and Blacksmithy still Locked at 100.0 each, Mining
Down at 99.7 and Tactics Locked at 0.3. The server's replacement audit recorded three tenths.

The temporary rats were deleted. After the client stopped, the disposable world saved and shut
down; the original anti-macro configuration and assembly list were restored, and the temporary
probe assembly and client credential file were removed. The source Skill Bank flag remains off.
The focused matrix covers one anti-macro target allowance, full-bank replacement, malformed-data recovery, and three live save/reload boundaries for under-cap restoration and full-cap exchange. Other skill-specific progression and time-driven Mastery award-period checks remain in the separate UOR player-skill audit; no additional Skill Bank ledger or ordinary-client case is open in this focused matrix.
