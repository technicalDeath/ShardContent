- **House lockdown reminder** (added 2026-10-08). A player who is in a house they own, puts an item down, and does not lock it down or secure it within about 10 seconds gets a reminder to do so. A cooldown keeps it from nagging: about once a day. *Not planned yet.* Things to settle in the plan: the cooldown per account or per character (the travel warning is per account); which items count (a lone item on the floor, not a container that is already secure, not trash or a placed addon); co-owners and friends (the request says "a house they own"); whether it also reminds for items put in an unsecured container; the words; a way to switch it off. What stock gives: `BaseHouse.LockDown(mobile, item)`, `AddSecure(mobile, item)`, `IsOwner`, `IsInside`, but the engine event for "an item was just dropped in a house" has not been looked for yet (it may need a narrow hook).

- **DONE 2026-10-11 (client defaults; uncommitted): the health bar of a dead monster cannot be dismissed** (owner's playtest report). Cause found in the client code, not reproduced by dragging a bar (the test tool cannot drag): a health bar that has snapped to another window ("anchored") ignores a plain right-click unless Alt is held, because the client option "hold ALT to close anchored gumps" is on by default (and is on in the owner's profile); and the client never closes the bar of a mobile that dies (`close_health_bar_type` 0). Both are now new-profile defaults (`hold_down_key_alt_to_close_anchored` false, `close_health_bar_type` 2, see `Client-Defaults.md`). The owner's own profile is unchanged: Alt + right-click closes an anchored bar today, or untick the option in Options.

- **Bola balls: do they have a use under our ruleset?** (owner, 2026-10-11, "for later"; ruling still wanted, see Rulings.) What the code says today: Orcs, Orc Bombers, Orc Brutes, Orcish Lords, Savages and Savage Riders carry a Bola Ball (20% of the time, stock files), and Tinkering crafts both the ball (10 iron ingots, Tinkering 45 to 95) and the Bola (4 balls and 3 leather, Tinkering 60 to 80); neither craft is era-gated in `DefTinkering`. What it does (UOGuide, Stratics): thrown at a mounted target it knocks them off their mount and stops them remounting for a few seconds; the thrower must be on foot; it drops where it lands. In our ruleset the only targets it matters against are mounted players, so its use is limited to consensual PvP (Hot Zones, players with Criminal Intent, criminals), not tested against Safe World. **Era check 2026-10-11: inconclusive.** Web searches found no source giving the publish that added the bola (or colored leather); the one date found is that Stratics's wiki lists the first Dull Copper Elemental sighting in 2000-08, so colored ore existed by August 2000. Leads for an exact date: archived uo.stratics.com pages from 1999 to 2000 and the 'UO:Renaissance Stratics Archive' thread on uorforum.com. Options: leave it (my recommendation while the date is unknown: low risk, PvP-only), remove the drop and the craft if it proves post-UOR, or restrict it to monsters.
