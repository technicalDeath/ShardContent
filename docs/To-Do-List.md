- **Bola balls: do they have a use under our ruleset?** (owner, 2026-10-11, "for later"; ruling still wanted, see Rulings.) What the code says today: Orcs, Orc Bombers, Orc Brutes, Orcish Lords, Savages and Savage Riders carry a Bola Ball (20% of the time, stock files), and Tinkering crafts both the ball (10 iron ingots, Tinkering 45 to 95) and the Bola (4 balls and 3 leather, Tinkering 60 to 80); neither craft is era-gated in `DefTinkering`. What it does (UOGuide, Stratics): thrown at a mounted target it knocks them off their mount and stops them remounting for a few seconds; the thrower must be on foot; it drops where it lands. In our ruleset the only targets it matters against are mounted players, so its use is limited to consensual PvP (Hot Zones, players with Criminal Intent, criminals), not tested against Safe World. **Era check 2026-10-11: inconclusive.** Web searches found no source giving the publish that added the bola (or colored leather); the one date found is that Stratics's wiki lists the first Dull Copper Elemental sighting in 2000-08, so colored ore existed by August 2000. Leads for an exact date: archived uo.stratics.com pages from 1999 to 2000 and the 'UO:Renaissance Stratics Archive' thread on uorforum.com. Options: leave it (my recommendation while the date is unknown: low risk, PvP-only), remove the drop and the craft if it proves post-UOR, or restrict it to monsters.
