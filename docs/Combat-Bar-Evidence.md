# Combat bar

Signed off 2026-10-12 by the owner ("Yes to all", Shift-click tooltip must mention itself). Client only: no server flag, no packet. On for new profiles (`combat_bar_enabled` in `data/client/default-profile.json`, pinned by `ClientDefaultsTests`).

## Design
- `CombatBarGump` (ClassicUO fork), just under the potion bar (below the game window when the potion bar is not showing; hidden when there is no room; stays where the player drags it).
- Slots: Bandages; Arrows, Bolts; the eight reagents; two equipment slots (the weapon or tool held last, and the one before it).
- Bandages: click = double-click the bandage and answer the target cursor with yourself; Shift-click = leave the cursor open. The tooltip says so.
- Equipment slots: click equips (taking off what blocks it: a two-handed weapon clears both hands, a one-handed one its own hand and any two-handed weapon), click on the one in hand puts it away. Serials are kept in the profile (`CombatBarLast`, `CombatBarPrevious`).
- Modes like the potion bar: every item (default), only the items you have, docked to one bandage icon (`CombatBarMode`).
- Counts read the pack tree on the client. Items in bags the client has not seen are known after the potion bar's silent bag opening at login.

## Three slots (2026-10-12, owner request)
Replaces the two "last / previous" slots: a main-hand weapon slot, a shield slot and a tool slot, each remembering the last item of its kind (`CombatBarMain`, `CombatBarShield`, `CombatBarTool`). A slot with no item shows its name (weapon, shield, tool); an item in hand shows `held`, one not in hand shows `equip`. Tools: pickaxe, hatchet (also an axe, so it goes here), shovel, fishing pole, smith's and sledge hammers, tongs. Spellbook and runebook are ignored. Conflicts: two-handed weapon or pole clears both hands; a shield clears the two-handed layer; a one-handed item clears the one-handed layer and any two-handed weapon or pole but keeps a shield.
Real client (disposable host, 2026-10-12): pickaxe goes to the tool slot while club and shield stay; spellbook in hand is ignored; club over a spellbook; fishing pole clears the shield; shield clears the pole; pole clears the shield; club clears the pole; shield clears a halberd. Not tested: hatchet, shovel, hammers, tongs, relog persistence of the three slots, other shields than the starter round shield and the Buckler.

## Item selector (2026-10-12, owner: "Make the item selector now")
Hold the left button on the weapon, shield or tool slot for 0.45 s without moving: a vertical strip opens above the slot (below it if there is no room) listing the matching items from the pack and bags that are not in hand (`EquipPickerGump`). A click on one equips it through the bar's own equip logic (clearing what is in the way). Seven rows at most, with "+N more" under them; the strip closes on a pick, a press anywhere else, a right click, or after 9 s. Releasing the opening press does not also equip or put away. Tooltips: the slots say "Hold the button to choose another" (empty slots "Hold to choose from your pack"); each row names the item.
Choices I made without asking: the 0.45 s hold; the strip stays open after the opening press is released (click to pick) instead of select-on-release; held items are left out of the list; sorted by name; the cap of seven.
Real client (disposable host, a pack with sixteen weapons, three shields, tools): weapon strip with seven rows and "+3 more"; shield strip with three; tool strip with three; picks that equipped: Buckler (shield over the starter shield), Broadsword (over the club), Fishing Pole (two-handed: cleared the sword and the shield), Kite Shield (over the pole), Broadsword again with the shield kept, Club from a fresh client; the tool slot remembered the pole across a relog. Two early picks in the first run did nothing and could not be reproduced afterwards (seven picks in a row worked, with and without tracing); I think they were the first picks after the strip was added but did not find a cause.
Not tested: a pick that fails on the server (too heavy, locked); items in a bag inside a bag; very long item names in the tooltip.

## Durability gauge and easier dismissal (2026-10-12, owner request)
- **Gauge:** a thin vertical bar on the right of each equipment slot and of each row in the selector, for items that have durability: green, with red filling from the top down as the item wears (`DrawDurability`). The era server sends no item properties (`opl.enable` is off), so there is a server half: extended command 0xA9, flag `combatBar` (activated 2026-10-12 on the owner's "activate"), `CombatBarService`: the client asks with up to eight serials (its three slots every 2.5 s, and the rows the selector lists), the server answers with hit points and maximum for weapons, armor and shields that are the asker's. Tools that count uses (shovel, tongs, hammers, fishing pole) have no hit points and get no gauge; a hatchet and pickaxe are axes and do. Tooltips add "Durability 12 of 38".
- **Dismissing the selector:** Escape, a left or right press anywhere outside it, the mouse wandering more than about 70 px from the strip and slot for 0.7 s, or 9 s idle; the press that closes it is not also a click on the bar (400 ms).
- **Evidence:** shard tests 1282 passed. Real client (disposable host with the flag on for the host only): slots showed full green, then Club 12 of 38 and Wooden Shield 5 of 22 showed mostly red with a little green at the bottom; the selector rows showed gauges (Broadsword 8, Kryss 15, Halberd 30). Each way of closing was checked by comparing a screenshot with the strip open and one after: Escape, press outside, mouse away, right click all closed it.
- **Not done:** the server half is not deployed and the flag is off, so players see no gauge until it is deployed and activated; gauges for items inside the pack outside the selector's rows; uses-remaining gauge for tools.

## The same cues on the standard item shortcut bar (2026-10-12, owner: "make sure these extra features also work on standard hotbar")
`CounterBarGump` slots (the stock item shortcut bar) now show the shard cues the combat bar has: a durability gauge on a weapon, shield or armor slot (asks the server, 2.5 s per slot, only for items whose art is a weapon or wearable), the count in red when a supply runs low (bandages and reagents 5 or fewer, ammunition 10 or fewer; other items are not judged, and the stock "highlight when below N" option still works), the wrong ammunition dimmed while a bow or crossbow is in hand, and a pulsing "heal" on a bandage slot while a bandage is being applied (`BandageWatch`, shared with the combat bar). Not copied: the hold-to-choose selector (a shortcut slot stands for one kind of item by art, not a hand slot), and self-targeting bandages (the stock bar leaves the target cursor to the player).
Real client (disposable host, a saved item shortcut bar with bandages, arrows, bolts, black pearl, broadsword, kryss, club and buckler; durability set to 8, 20, club full and 3): gauges on the four weapons and the shield, red counts on 8 arrows and 3 pearls, yellow on 12 bolts and 50 bandages, "heal" with the slot highlighted after double-clicking the bandage slot and targeting yourself, the count back to normal (49) afterwards; with a bow held the bolts cell measured darker (65.3 to 55.6) and the arrows cell did not change.
Limits: if the player cancels the bandage target cursor the cue stays until the estimate runs out (about 8 to 10 s); the gauge needs the server half (flag `combatBar`, off, not deployed), so nothing shows until that is on.
