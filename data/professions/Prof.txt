# Britannia Renaissance character-creation templates: the Ultima Online professions of August 1999 (Publish 8), in force
# through Renaissance (April 2000). Adventurer and Merchant folders (two levels) and Advanced. Each profession is three skills and
# 80 stat points. The era's 100 skill points are scaled proportionally to 120 (as Advanced gives 120), no skill above 50, by the owner's decision. One source of truth for the server and the distributed client.
# Source of the values: the UO Second Age forum reconstruction of the era Prof.txt (not an Origin file); see Beta-1-Character-Templates-Audit.md.
# Field Medic and Battle Mage are left out by the owner's decision.
# Tab-separated. The server (ModernUO ProfessionInfo) reads TrueName, Desc (the profession ID sent in the creation packet), Skill and Stat
# from blocks whose Type is Profession and ignores folders; the client also reads Name, DescText, Gump, Type Category, TopLevel and Children.
# Profession IDs 8 to 23 are the shard's. IDs 1 to 7 are deliberately unused, so a client that sends one falls back to Advanced.
# Folders list their parents before their children, and a folder's Type comes before any other server key (the server stops reading a block at a non-profession Type).
